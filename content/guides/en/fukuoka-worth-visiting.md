## An honest question deserves an interested party's honest data

"Is Fukuoka worth visiting?" gets asked constantly — usually answered by bloggers who spent 36 hours here once.

We have more skin in the game: we run two whole-house rentals in central Fukuoka. And we have something better than an opinion — **booking records: 304 groups, 1,528 guests, from more than 10 countries** (measured August 2026). People vote on whether a city is worth visiting with flights, nights and return trips. Here is how they voted.

## The verdict hiding in our records

| What 1,528 guests actually did (Aug 2026) | |
|---|---|
| Countries represented | **10+** (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korea, Japan, Thailand, Singapore, UK, Australia, Canada, France…) |
| Average stay | **2.5 nights** |
| Taiwanese guests | **about 3 nights** |
| UK / Australia / Singapore guests | 3–5 nights |
| Average group | about 5 people — families and friend groups |

Read that middle line again. Travelers from Taiwan — two hours away, spoiled for choice, mostly Japan veterans who have already done Tokyo and Osaka — fly in and stay **three nights**. You do not do that for a boring city. You do that for a city you intend to enjoy slowly and revisit.

## Honest inventory: what Fukuoka doesn't have — and what it does

**Doesn't have**: a Golden Pavilion. A floating torii gate. A theme park worth flying for. If your Japan trip is a checklist of icons, Kyoto and Hiroshima will serve you better. We mean that.

**Does have**:
- **Japan's best eating city, per yen**: yatai stalls, birthplace-grade tonkotsu ramen, motsunabe, mizutaki, morning fish markets — at prices well under Tokyo's
- **Japan's most convenient airport**: about 5 minutes by subway to Hakata Station. Your first and last days are not "transfer days" — unique among major Japanese cities
- **A walkable core**: Tenjin, Hakata and the Nakasu stalls are all on foot or a short taxi
- **A city built for staying, not sightseeing**: shop a morning market, cook in your own kitchen, walk the riverside at dusk. The pleasure here is the top layer of everyday life, not a queue at a monument

Fukuoka is a weak "sightseeing city" and a world-class "**staying city**." Every disappointed review you will ever read about Fukuoka was written by someone who measured it with the wrong ruler.
