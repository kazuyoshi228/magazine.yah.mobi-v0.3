## 'I'm in Japan, but ChatGPT won't open' — yes, this really happens

You're travelling in Japan, your eSIM shows full signal, and yet ChatGPT is the one thing that won't load — or your Instagram post keeps failing to upload. It's a complaint that comes up again and again on social media and travel forums.

The cause isn't your signal, and it isn't your phone — it's **which country that eSIM routes your traffic through**. Travel eSIMs are sold all over the world, and some of the cheapest ones carry your traffic to Japan by way of a mainland China or other third-country network. Your phone may be sitting in Japan, but **as far as the internet is concerned, you're 'connecting from China' or 'connecting from Hong Kong'**.

## Why it fails — what the routing point changes

| eSIM routing point (IP country) | ChatGPT | Instagram/TikTok | Japan-facing services | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Routes through mainland China** | ❌ Blocked | ❌ Blocked | △ | China's access restrictions apply just as they do inside China |
| Third country such as Hong Kong or Singapore | ○ Usually works | ○ | **△ Region checks misfire** | Rejected by streaming/booking sites; higher latency |
| **Japan IP (local connection)** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Same conditions as being inside Japan |

There are two distinct levels to keep in mind.

1. **Mainland China routing = flat-out blocked**. ChatGPT, Google and most major social apps other than LINE simply won't work. This is the classic story behind 'I bought a cheap eSIM and every social app died'.
2. **Third-country routing = subtly off**. ChatGPT may run fine, but Japan-facing video streaming, ticket booking and some bank or payment sites can turn you away as 'overseas access'. This is actually the more common way to get tripped up while travelling.

## Before-you-buy checklist for spotting one

Check the following on the purchase page.

- [ ] **Does it state 'Japan IP', 'Japanese local line' or 'local breakout'?** — If it's spelled out, it's a Japan IP
- [ ] Can you confirm it 'does not route through a Chinese carrier'? — Be wary if there's no mention and the price is unusually cheap
- [ ] Do the reviews mention 'ChatGPT doesn't work' or 'my social apps got blocked'?
- [ ] Still unsure? Ask the seller one question: **'Which country will this eSIM's IP address be in?'** — A seller who can't answer on the spot is best avoided

Confirming it after activation is just as simple: open an IP-check site in your browser (search 'what is my ip'), and **if the country shown is 'Japan', you're good**. If anything other than Japan appears, that eSIM is routing through a third country.
